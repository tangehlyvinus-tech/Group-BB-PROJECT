Computer Science Department

The Computer Science Department at GHS Mbonjo is dedicated to equipping students with essential digital skills for the 21st century. Our curriculum covers:

  • Introduction to Computers – Hardware, software, and operating systems.
  • Programming Basics – Python, JavaScript, and algorithmic thinking.
  • Web Development – HTML, CSS, and building responsive websites.
  • Networking – Concepts of LAN, WAN, and internet protocols.
  • Database Management – SQL and data organisation.
  • ICT for Society – Ethical use of technology, cybersecurity, and digital citizenship.

Our well-equipped computer lab provides hands-on experience, and our qualified teachers mentor students to participate in coding competitions, tech fairs, and national ICT initiatives. We aim to produce tech-savvy graduates ready for higher education and the job market.
