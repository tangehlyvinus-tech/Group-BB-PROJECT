About GHS Mbonjo

Government High School (GHS) Mbonjo Limbe is a government-run secondary school located in Bimbia, within the Limbe III Subdivision, Fako Division, South West Region of Cameroon. Established on November 3, 1975, the school began its operations in the derelict packing stores of the former Public Works Department (PWD) at Down Beach–Mbonjo, Limbe.

At its inception, GHS Mbonjo welcomed 103 pioneering students (65 boys and 38 girls) under visionary leadership committed to providing quality secondary education to the youth of the region.

Over the years, the school grew steadily in both population and impact. Due to persistent flooding and deteriorating conditions at the Down Beach location, the decision was made—under the directive of the Honourable Minister of Secondary Education—to relocate the school to Bimbia, where it currently operates in a more secure and conducive learning environment.

Our Mission

To provide holistic education that promotes academic excellence, discipline, and moral integrity in students, preparing them for responsible citizenship and global competitiveness.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in the South West Region of Cameroon, recognized for quality teaching, innovative programs, and a nurturing environment that empowers learners to reach their full potential.

What We Offer

  • General Secondary Education (1st Cycle and 2nd Cycle)
  • Dedicated and Qualified Staff
  • Vibrant Student Clubs and Associations
  • ICT Integration and Digital Literacy
  • Moral and Civic Education
  • Safe and Supportive Learning Environment

Today, GHS Mbonjo Limbe remains a pillar of academic excellence and personal development in the Limbe III community. Our students consistently distinguish themselves in public exams, extracurricular activities, and national programs.

We take pride in our legacy—and we are committed to building a future where every student receives the tools, guidance, and support needed to succeed in school and in life.
